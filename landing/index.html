04 / FIELD NOTES

Questions, answered without the noise.

Many of the toolkit's workflows are designed to run locally and remain useful without a cloud dependency. Networked or device-specific features behave differently when disconnected.
Standard crypto and utilities work across all modern Android versions. Advanced system tools like Shizuku bridge or Package Eradication require Shizuku or root privileges.
The product is positioned around local-first handling: your working data, keys, notes, and files are intended to stay on the device during supported offline workflows.
Android power users, security researchers, system administrators, and crypto enthusiasts who operate in offline or air-gapped environments.
